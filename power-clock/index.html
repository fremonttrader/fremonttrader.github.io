Power Clock Screensaver

Turn your TV into an elegant clock display with 30 professional faces, from animated flip clocks to classic analog and world clocks.

A clock for your room

Choose a clean digital clock for your desk, an animated flip clock for your living room, or a world clock display for your office or hotel lobby. Power Clock Screensaver is designed for Roku TVs and streaming players, with settings you control using your Roku remote.

  • 30 distinct faces: 25 clock designs plus 5 hotel and international layouts, including a world map.
  • Flip, digital and analog clocks: animated flip panels, seven-segment numbers, classic hands and a dedicated 24-hour dial with 0–22 numerals.
  • World clocks: show up to six selected cities in supported international layouts, with time zones and daylight-saving rules.
  • Your display preferences: choose 12-hour or 24-hour digital time, show or hide seconds and the date, and adjust clock size and animation.
  • Color choices: select a palette and Default, Green or Red time numbers where the clock face supports them. Classic analog dials retain their designed colors.
  • Night mode: dim the clock continuously or on a schedule. Your style and settings are saved on your Roku device.

Set up your screensaver

After installing Power Clock Screensaver on your Roku:

  1. Open Settings → Theme → Screensavers → My screensavers.
  2. Select Power Clock Screensaver, then choose Set as screensaver.
  3. Open Change screensaver settings. Browse Clock styles and press OK to save your choice.
  4. Adjust Display, Night mode and Time zones as desired.
  5. Choose Full-screen preview to see your design on the TV.

Power Clock is managed in Roku’s Screensavers menu. It does not appear as a regular app tile on the Home screen. Menu wording may vary with your Roku OS version.

Choose when it starts. Set the delay in Settings → Theme → Screensaver wait time. This is a Roku setting; Power Clock shows guidance but does not change it. Roku controls when screensavers run. Press a remote button to leave the running screensaver.

Get the display you want

The flip numbers are not moving

Open Display and turn Clock animation on. Turn Show seconds on for a visible change every second. Hour and minute panels flip when their values change.

The clock is too bright or too dim

Check Night mode and its schedule and dimming level. Night mode dims the clock drawing; it does not change your TV’s hardware brightness. You can also use your TV’s picture settings.

The time or city is wrong

Check your Roku’s time zone and Power Clock’s Time zones settings. Local time follows the Roku device clock. International layouts use your selected cities; the number shown depends on the layout.

The display stops overnight

Your TV’s sleep timer, power-saving settings or another app can end the display. Power Clock does not override those controls or keep the TV awake. Night mode is a display setting; this version has no wake-up alarms.

Simple, local settings

The current app includes its clock artwork and time-zone data. Its clock display does not make network requests, and the app has no account sign-in, advertisements or analytics. Preferences stay on your device. Roku’s own services, updates and installation have their own connection requirements and privacy practices.
