Privacy policy

1. About this policy

Power Clock Screensaver is a standalone Roku screensaver published by Fremont Trader (“we,” “us,” or “the publisher”). This policy covers the app, these supporting web pages, and information you choose to send us for support.

The current app works locally. Its clock faces, artwork, fonts, and time-zone rules are bundled in the app. The app has no sign-in, advertising or analytics integration, and does not make internet requests or send your settings to us. Website visits, support email, and Roku’s own platform services have separate information handling described below.

2. Information used on your Roku

The app uses the date and time available on your Roku, its local time-zone offset, its 12-hour or 24-hour clock preference, and its screensaver wait-time setting to display and configure the clock. It does not use GPS or determine your physical location. A city or time zone you select is a display preference.

The app does not request your name, email address, Roku account credentials, payment details, device serial number, advertising identifier, unique device identifier, or IP address through device APIs. It does not access a camera, microphone, contacts, or personal files.

A local startup diagnostic message records whether the screensaver or settings view is running and the selected style number. The app does not upload this message to the publisher.

3. Settings stored on your device

Power Clock saves preferences in the app’s local storage on your Roku: clock style, colors, time format, seconds and date visibility, clock size, selected time zone and cities, night-mode schedule and dimming level, animation, gentle position shift, and map visibility.

We do not receive a copy of these preferences or provide a cloud account, backup, synchronization, or remote settings-management service. Anyone with access to your Roku may be able to see or change the preferences.

Preferences remain locally until changed or the relevant Roku storage is cleared. To change them, open Settings → Theme → Screensavers → My screensavers → Power Clock Screensaver → Change screensaver settings. The app does not include a separate erase-all-preferences control, and we cannot retrieve or remotely erase local settings. Removing the screensaver and clearing device storage are managed by Roku; a reinstall is not promised to reset every preference.

4. Roku platform services

Roku independently provides the device operating system, time synchronization, app installation and updates, and Streaming Store. Roku may process platform, usage, and diagnostic information under the Roku Privacy Policy. The app’s local operation does not disable these separate platform services.

There are no app-owned network requests in the current release. If a future release introduces hosted artwork or other connections, we will update the policy to describe that behavior.

5. Website visits

These static information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms, or cookies to the Power Clock pages. Your browser requests the page, stylesheet, and images from the website host.

GitHub receives ordinary connection and request information and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and Privacy Statement. If you access these pages through fremonttrader.com, Cloudflare also delivers the website and may process connection and request information under its Privacy Policy. The Roku app does not load these web pages in the background.

6. Email support and retention

If you email support@fremonttrader.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, and maintain related support records. Our email and hosting providers process information needed to deliver their services.

We retain support correspondence as reasonably needed to handle the request and follow-up, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell support correspondence or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to address security or protect legal rights. Service providers may process information outside your country.

Please share only what is needed. Do not send passwords, account details, serial numbers, or unnecessary personal information. Redact screenshots and logs before sending them.

7. Privacy requests and security

Contact support@fremonttrader.com to ask about information you have sent us or to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to applicable law. We may need to verify that a request relates to you. Local clock preferences must be managed on your Roku device; we do not hold a server-side copy. For information held independently by Roku, GitHub, or Cloudflare, contact the relevant provider.

No storage or communications system is completely secure. Avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your support request.

8. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when the app or supporting services change. We will update the effective date and provide any additional notice required by applicable law. The current version will remain available on this page.
