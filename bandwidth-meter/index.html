Bandwidth Meter

Check download speed, upload speed, and latency directly on your Roku TV or streaming player.

Understand your connection

Run a test with your Roku remote, choose a download server, and review your 20 most recent completed results on your device. The app is free, with no app account, subscription, or in-app purchase.

Results reflect the connection between your Roku and the test services. Network conditions, device capabilities, and server availability affect the measurements. Tests transfer data and may count toward your internet plan’s allowance.
