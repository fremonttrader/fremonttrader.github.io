3. Understanding test results

Download speed, upload speed, latency, and related results are estimates of the connection between your Roku device and the test services during that test. They are affected by device capabilities, Wi-Fi or Ethernet conditions, other network activity, routing, selected servers, and service availability.

Results are provided for general information. They are not a certification of your internet plan, a guaranteed measure of your connection’s maximum capacity, or a guarantee of streaming quality. A result may differ from a test on another device or from another testing service. The app is not intended for safety-critical, emergency, or other uses that require guaranteed network performance.

6. Privacy and saved results

The Privacy Policy explains automatic network-information lookups, test-service requests, local history, and website hosting. Opening the app initiates an IP-information lookup even before a speed test begins.

Recent test results are stored locally on your Roku device, with a maximum of 20 completed records. You can delete them using History → Clear history. We do not provide a cloud backup or recovery service for that history. Do not rely on the app as your sole archive of important measurements.
