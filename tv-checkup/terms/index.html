3. Understanding the checks

The app provides visual and listening references. You judge the results and record your own observations. The app does not automatically diagnose faults, repair a device, change TV settings, measure hearing, or certify equipment performance.

Picture patterns use SDR and do not perform instrument-based or HDR calibration. The flash-and-beep check does not automatically measure delay in milliseconds. Sound diagrams and waveforms illustrate test signals; they are not microphone measurements.

Surround and subwoofer routing depend on your Roku device, audio connection, decoder, and speaker setup. Signals may be mixed down, and selecting a 5.1 or 2.1 layout does not prove that your equipment will produce discrete output from every selected speaker.

Device information depends on available Roku readings and model specifications. Some fields may be unavailable, incomplete, or outdated. On a player or streaming soundbar, CPU and memory details describe that Roku device. Installed-memory specifications and the app’s available-memory readings have different meanings.

4. Responsible use

Use the app on equipment you own or are authorized to use, and follow your equipment manufacturer’s instructions. Begin sound checks at a comfortable low volume. Adjust device volume carefully, limit extended tones and repeated patterns, and stop if the sound or image causes discomfort.

Test-signal level controls are relative levels within the app, not calibrated sound-pressure measurements. You remain responsible for your TV and audio-system volume settings. Do not use the checks as the sole basis for safety-critical, medical, repair, or warranty decisions.
