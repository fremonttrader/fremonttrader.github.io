Privacy policy

A clear explanation of information used by the app, what stays on your device, and the choices available to you.

1. About this policy

TV Checkup is a Roku utility published by Fremont Trader (“we,” “us,” or “the publisher”). It provides guided picture and sound checks, a device report, and a checklist for your own observations.

This policy covers information handled by TV Checkup, these app information pages, and support requests. The app displays device information and manages observations on your Roku. It does not upload your device report or saved observations to us.

Version information. Version 1.0.15 includes all test sounds in the app. The upcoming version 1.0.16 will retrieve selected Sound Checks samples from GitHub Pages during playback. The sound-request disclosures below apply to version 1.0.16; its Sound Checks will require an internet connection.

2. Information used on your device

To display the Device Report and choose supported checks, the app reads information made available by your Roku device: model and manufacturer, Roku OS version, display characteristics, audio and decoder capabilities, supported features, connection type and status, and app-memory information.

Where available, CPU and installed-memory details come from model specifications included in the app. Looking up these specifications does not contact a remote service. Updates to the bundled specifications arrive with app updates.

The app does not read your Roku account email, advertising identifier, device serial number, unique device identifier, IP or MAC address, Wi-Fi network name, contacts, camera, or microphone through device APIs. An internet request still exposes the connection IP address to the host, as explained below. It does not scan your installed apps or personal files.

3. Saved observations and settings

The app stores your choices for ten checklist observations in its local storage on the Roku device. An observation can be Looks good, Needs attention, or Not checked. No free-text notes, account details, or copies of the Device Report are saved in that checklist.

Sound-test settings are used during the app session. Version 1.0.16 will use Roku’s normal media playback buffering for hosted sounds; it will not provide a permanent sound download or an offline sample library. We do not operate a server, cloud backup, or synchronization service for your reports or observations. Anyone with access to your Roku device may be able to view or change the saved observations.

4. Sound playback and Roku services

Version 1.0.15 includes its sound samples in the app. In version 1.0.16, Sound Checks will request the selected authored sound samples over HTTPS from fremonttrader.github.io, our GitHub Pages host. The request identifies the sample file, including its test signal, speaker route, and signal level. The host receives ordinary connection and request information, including the connection IP address and requested file. The app will not add account identifiers, device identifiers, your Device Report, or saved observation results to these requests. These requests deliver playback media; we do not add analytics or advertising events. There is no app sign-in, advertising integration, or in-app purchase system.

Picture checks, the separate picture and sound sync reference, guidance, model specifications, and saved observations remain local. Sound Checks in version 1.0.16 will require an internet connection while playing hosted samples; playback can be unavailable when the host or connection is unavailable.

Roku independently operates the device, operating system, app installation and updates, and Streaming Store. The app uses Roku’s standard launch-completion signal. Roku may process platform and diagnostic information under the Roku Privacy Policy. Local checks do not disable the device’s separate platform services.

5. Retention and your choices

  • Clear one observation: open My checkup, select the item, press OK, choose Not checked, then press OK to save.
  • Clear all observations: open My checkup → Options (*) → Clear all, then confirm.
  • Stop a sound check: use Stop or Back. Exit the app when you have finished.

Saved observations remain in the app’s local storage until you change or clear them, or that storage is otherwise reset. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase them. Clearing the checklist does not delete a support email you previously sent or records held independently by Roku or website providers.

6. This website and support requests

These static pages and the upcoming version 1.0.16 sound samples are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, contact forms, or cookies to the TV Checkup pages. GitHub receives connection and request information when a page or sound file is requested and logs IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Pages documentation and Privacy Statement. The upcoming app’s sound requests will use the GitHub Pages address directly. If you visit these pages through fremonttrader.com, Cloudflare also delivers the website and may process connection and request information under its Privacy Policy.

If you email support@fremonttrader.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate reported problems, and maintain support records. Email and hosting providers process the information needed to deliver those services. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably needed to handle the request, related follow-up, security, and legal obligations.

We do not sell support correspondence or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to address security or protect legal rights. Service providers may process information outside your country.

Share only what is needed. Do not send passwords, account details, serial numbers, or unnecessary personal information. Redact screenshots and logs before sending them. If you choose to use a public GitHub issue instead of email, your username and submission are publicly visible under GitHub’s controls and policies.

7. Privacy requests and security

Contact us at support@fremonttrader.com to ask about information you have sent us or to request access, correction, or deletion. We will handle requests according to applicable law and may need to verify that the request relates to you. Local observations must be managed on your Roku device using the controls above. Requests about records held independently by Roku, GitHub, or Cloudflare should be directed to those providers.

No storage or communications system is completely secure. TV Checkup is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. It does not ask for personal profiles or age information. A parent or guardian who believes a child sent us personal information can contact us to request its removal.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app, website, or support practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. We will describe material changes in the updated policy and provide any additional notice required by law. This policy does not limit privacy rights available under applicable law.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or TV Checkup’s data handling, contact Fremont Trader at support@fremonttrader.com. Please use “TV Checkup privacy” in the subject.
