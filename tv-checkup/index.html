TV Checkup

Get to know your TV with guided picture checks, speaker tests, and a detailed Roku device report.

A useful check, an honest result

Use your Roku remote to explore your picture and sound, learn about your device, and keep your own observations. TV Checkup runs on compatible Roku TVs, streaming players, and streaming soundbars. Its checks work offline, with no app login, ads, or subscription.

Explore the app

  • Picture checks: screen fit, dark detail, bright detail, color bars, gray steps, and solid-color uniformity screens, with on-screen guidance.
  • Sound checks: speaker identification, frequency sweeps, and fixed tones. Choose stereo (2.0), stereo plus subwoofer (2.1), front left/center/right, or 5.1. Select speakers and adjust each test-signal level in 10% steps, with duration, repeat, and playback controls.
  • Picture and sound sync: a flash-and-beep reference to help you judge whether what you see and hear feels synchronized.
  • Device report: browse system, processor, memory, display, audio, network, and supported-feature information. CPU and installed-memory specifications are shown where available for your Roku model.
  • My checkup: save observations as Looks good or Needs attention, revisit them, and clear them on the device.
  • How to use: guidance on the checks, remote controls, and the limits of the results.

Make the most of your checks

Start sound tests at a comfortable low volume and adjust your TV or audio system with its normal volume controls. Actual surround and subwoofer output depends on your Roku, audio equipment, connections, and settings; some systems mix multichannel signals into stereo.

You judge the picture and sound yourself. TV Checkup does not automatically diagnose faults, repair pixels, calibrate HDR, change TV settings, or measure audio delay in milliseconds. Picture patterns are SDR. Hardware information varies by model; on a streaming player or soundbar, processor and memory details describe that Roku device.
